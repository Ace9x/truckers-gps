Privacy at RigRoute GPS

Last updated October 22, 2026

RigRoute is a free truck navigation web app. It works without an account. We don't sell data and there are no ads or trackers.

Your location

Used on your phone to show your position, plan truck-safe routes and warn you about hazards ahead. Your position is not stored on our servers. It leaves your phone only in these cases:

  • Route requests (start, stops, destination and your truck's size) go to Valhalla routing servers run by the OpenStreetMap community (valhalla1.openstreetmap.de).
  • Searches go to Photon (photon.komoot.io), an OpenStreetMap geocoder.
  • Truck stops, weigh stations and restrictions along your route are looked up from Overpass (OpenStreetMap) using the route area.
  • If you turn on Share my speed, anonymous speed samples are published to Nostr relays under a random key.
  • If you share with friends, your live position goes only to friends you approved, only while sharing is on.

What syncs and what stays on your phone

DataWhere it goes
Truck profile, display name, pointsYour RigRoute account (Supabase) if you sign in; otherwise this phone only
Hazard reports and votesPublic to other drivers via Supabase and Nostr relays, signed with a random driver key, not your name
LoadsOnly the dispatcher you paired with
Routes, stops, trips and receipts, HOS log, facility notes, settingsThis phone only

Map and traffic data

Map tiles come from OpenFreeMap. Traffic speeds come from NYC DOT open data. These requests reveal the map area you view, like any map website.

Export or delete

In the app, open Me > Privacy. Export my data downloads everything stored on your phone as JSON. Delete all data erases it and signs you out. Hazard reports already sent to public relays can't be recalled. To delete your cloud account, sign in and use Delete all, then contact us on GitHub and we will delete the server profile.

Safety notice

RigRoute is not a certified ELD. Truck restrictions come from OpenStreetMap and may be incomplete. Always obey posted signs.
