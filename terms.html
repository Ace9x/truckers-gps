Routes are guidance only

RigRoute suggests routes from OpenStreetMap, Valhalla and NYC DOT open data. That data can be wrong, missing or out of date. You are responsible for your route. Always obey posted signs, bridge and tunnel clearances, weight limits, hazmat restrictions and police directions. RigRoute does not guarantee that any route is legal or safe for your vehicle, load or permits.

Not an ELD

The hours-of-service tools are a planning aid only. RigRoute is not a registered electronic logging device and is not a record of duty status under 49 CFR Part 395. Use your carrier's FMCSA-registered ELD (eld.fmcsa.dot.gov).

Don't use the phone while driving

FMCSA rules bar commercial drivers from holding or texting on a phone while driving; only single-touch use of a mounted device is allowed (49 CFR 392.80 and 392.82). New York also bans hand-held phone use while driving (VTL §1225-c, §1225-d). RigRoute locks typing, forms, the freight board and multi-step screens above 5 mph and keeps only voice and single-tap controls. Mount your phone and set up the trip before you move.

Vehicle types

  • Car: normal car routing, no truck rules.
  • Cargo van / Reefer van: if your van has commercial plates it is a commercial vehicle under NYC Traffic Rules §4-01(b) and may not use NYC parkways (Belt, FDR, Henry Hudson, Cross Island, Grand Central and others). RigRoute keeps vans off parkways. Vans with 2 axles/4 tires are not "trucks" under §4-13 and need not follow the Truck Route Network, but must obey commercial-vehicle signs.
  • Box truck / Semi: trucks (2 axles and 6 tires, or 3+ axles) must use the NYC Truck Route Network (34 RCNY §4-13). Through trips use Through Truck Routes.
  • Hazmat: placarded loads must follow federal hazmat routing (49 CFR Part 397) and posted tunnel/bridge bans, including the NYC river crossings that bar hazmat. Set your hazmat class in vehicle settings; still check your permits and routing designations.
